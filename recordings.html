How recording works

Record while listening in your signed-in website. No additional sign-in is needed. Recording happens in real time and includes all sound from the selected tab. Keep Chrome and the source tab open.

Enable “Start recording only when audio is playing” to wait for Chrome’s audio-playing status. No audio file is created while waiting. Muted tabs wait until unmuted. Chrome’s status may lag slightly; enable the separate “Stop and save when audio finishes” option to save automatically to Downloads when Chrome reports silence. Pauses or buffering can trigger automatic saving too.

Closing the popup or switching to another tab is fine. If the source tab closes, the recorder finishes and retains the audio here. After a browser interruption, previously stored chunks can be downloaded, but the file may be incomplete.

Files use WebM with Opus audio. Open them in Chrome or a compatible player such as VLC. These are recordings of playback, not copies of the original MP3 or AAC file.
