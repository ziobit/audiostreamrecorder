What can these measurements tell me?

The AudioContext rate describes Chrome’s captured playback path. Chrome may already have resampled or mixed the source.

A repeated high-frequency cutoff can suggest limited signal bandwidth. Quiet passages, instruments, filters, mixing and lossy encoding can all affect it. This is a threshold-based observation, not a quality score.

Neither the cutoff nor the capture rate identifies the original sample rate, codec or bitrate. The requested and measured bitrates describe only this new recording.
